# 13. Record Control

This Master Record should be updated whenever any of the following occurs:

- payment is confirmed;
- invoice or statement is received;
- credit is identified;
- change order is proposed or executed;
- insurance payment or adjustment occurs;
- supporting invoice/subcontractor record is received;
- material scope clarification occurs;
- walkthrough establishes completion or noncompletion;
- final inspection occurs;
- project reaches substantial/final completion.

Changes should preserve prior figures and dates rather than overwrite historical representations.

The governing principle of this record is:

> **Document what is established. Identify what is unresolved. Reconcile before concluding.**
